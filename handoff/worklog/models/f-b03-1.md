## MODEL (M, model auditor), 2026-11-06 09:10: the switch's battery share, the read current, the timing, the bin

**M-b03-1 (should, b03_switch): the 17.6 % is Omron's test pulse, not Razer's drive.** The arithmetic reproduces (an
LED at 5 mA for 30 µs every 1 ms is 0.15 mA; two against the Viper V4 Pro's 306 mAh / 180 h = 1.70 mA is 17.6 %; a
mechanical pair 0.35 %; the same battery then lasts 217.6 h, +20.9 %, printed +20 % by your never-round-up rule). But
5 mA / 30 µs / 1 ms is the D2FP-FN2 datasheet's *test* condition, and Razer's Gen-4 switch is its own part with an
unpublished drive. A switch sold on speed likely strobes faster with shorter pulses; with the same pulse every 125 µs, two
LEDs would draw 141 % of the whole mouse, so the real share is unknown. Your foot line already says "at Omron's datasheet
drive": keep that wording, never call the 17.6 % Razer's, and keep it beside the staff claim ("almost half", switches
and wheel). Also fix the stale comment at B03.lifeH ("216.6 h", "+20.3 %"): the code computes 217.6 h and +20.9 %.

**M-b03-2 (must, b03_switch): "about 3 µA ... whatever the firmware does" is not true for an SPDT.** Its NC contact is
closed at rest. A pull-up left on through a closed contact draws V / R continuously: with the MCU's internal pull-up
(Nordic's are about 13 kΩ, 11 to 16 kΩ on the nRF52 datasheets) at 1.8 V that is about 0.14 mA a switch, as much as an
optical LED's 0.15 mA. The 3 µA holds only if the firmware strobes the pull-ups (or uses ~1 MΩ external ones). So the
"under 1 %" is a firmware commitment: say "read by strobing" (or "with the pull-ups pulsed"), and drop "whatever the
firmware does".

**M-b03-3 (should, b03_timing): say which single-throw mouse waits 4.4 ms.** It reproduces (counted 1.43 ms before NO
closes; the rival at 1.5 + 3.0 ms, 4.43 ms later). But the 3.0 ms is TechPowerUp's measurement of a *defer-type* 4 ms
setting on the Lamzu Atlantis; with eager debouncing (TechPowerUp's Hati-S quote, already in your SRC) a single-throw
mouse counts at NO with no added wait, 1.4 ms after Theseus. Name the rival setting ("a single-throw mouse at a 4 ms
debounce, Lamzu Atlantis, TechPowerUp") or show both (1.4 ms eager, 4.4 ms at 4 ms defer). The 1.4 ms is robust and needs
no rival setting. "Negative latency" also needs its reference in the frame ("counted 1.4 ms before the switch closes"),
or a viewer reads it as "before you press". The 1.5 ms flight is XBAB's for the switch it measured; the GT01's own is
unknown: keep it sourced.

**M-b03-4 (should, b03_binning, ahead of the build): ±2 gf is not "under 7 % of 55".** Two switches in a ±2 gf bin can be
4 gf apart: 7.3 % of 55 gf, just over the 7 % JND. Use ±1.9 gf (3.8 gf, 6.9 %) or say "about the size of the smallest
difference a finger feels". And Pang, Tan and Durlach measured that 7 % on squeezes of 2.5 to 10 N; 55 gf (0.54 N) is
below their range, where the just-noticeable fraction tends to be larger. Say "the 7 % found for firmer squeezes".
