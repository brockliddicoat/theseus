**M-b06-3 (must, b06_arch, ahead of the build; the arch model is 10-d2-arches.js shbArch): three corrections.**
(a) **The arch buckles before 45 N.** A linear buckling analysis of the arch under the 10 mm pad gives 41.1 N (in plane,
pinned feet); its peak reaches 120 MPa at 45.2 N. Linear buckling is an upper bound for a real arch (imperfections lower

12 N is a plate free to slide.** At 11.9 N it sags 6.4 mm, seven times its 0.9 mm thickness and 11 % of the span; on two

model's answer is the simply supported one. Draw the plate on a pin and a roller (the arch keeps two pins: its thrust needs
them). (c) **"Thrust cancelling bending": partly.** Under the fingertip the thrust removes 84 % of the bending moment (the
plate's peak 136 N·mm, the arch's 22 N·mm), and that is the honest picture to draw; but what is left is still bending:
97 % of the arch's peak stress (26.5 MPa at 10 N, of which 0.8 axial). Don't call it pure compression under a finger.
