asked what radius scatter keeps the peak within ±5 %: Δr = 0.05 · SCF / |dSCF/dr|. That reproduces: about 0.013 mm near a
0.05 mm corner, 0.24 mm at 0.6 mm, so 18 to 19×. The idea is sound (a fillet sits where the curve is flat, so tool wear and
polish barely move its peak). Two cautions if b06_mold reuses it: the 19× is measured against the sharpest radius, where
the FE's stress concentration is least reliable (a sharp corner is singular and its value follows the mesh), and it moves
with the reference (against a 0.1 mm corner it is about 15×). Say "against a 0.05 mm corner" or quote the 0.1 mm figure;
at the curve's minimum (0.75 to 0.9 mm) the allowed scatter is larger still, so "at 0.6 mm" is the cautious place to read it.
