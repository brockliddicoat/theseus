   popping, and creaking" is about the later ULX Competition; Finalmouse and Periview call the Competition solid-sided, and
   owners are split. So: b01's row must name one model, with that model's price, weight and fault, each sourced; and
   b05_fallacy must not use the Competition as the "built better" example unless it also shows RTINGS's finding against
   it (which breaks the example). The callback in BROADCAST-0930 (F17) only stands if the facts support it; otherwise drop

3. Human-scale forces (M5). No measurement of grip or press force during gaming exists (Q2, seven queries). Measured office
   figures: a squeeze of 0.4 to 1 N on average, peaks about 2 N (Johnson 2000; Won 2009); a resting hand weighs about 4.4 N

   sugar, about five times the hardest grip measured at a desk"), never "a firm press" or "typical"; a resting palm is 4.4 N.
   Anything you print as "the hardest a hand can squeeze" needs a source or goes.
