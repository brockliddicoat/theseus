## R1: The intro: every flagship compromises (30-r1-intro.js, 10 slides)

- `r1_cover` (intro) **Theseus: zero compromises**: The promise in one frame: the lightest flagship-class mouse that gives up nothing. PIAKNOW'S in capitals; "zero compromises" where 8 khz was; density where "100+ ideas" was. Replaces: `cover`.
- `r1_compromise` (intro) **Every flagship compromises**: The problem that the whole film answers: each flagship trades something away (battery for weight, durability for weight, an optical switch's battery for speed, shape for weight, price). A table or map of named flagships, each with its own sourced compromise, and Theseus with none. Derive every blank; one precision per column; the Superstrike X3 is a flagship (the GPX 2 less so). Replaces: `flagships`, `thesis`, `market`.
- `r1_targets` (intro) **The targets**: What zero compromises means in numbers: Dual 8khz wired and wireless, press to PC (shorter: the click work cuts mechanical latency), weight headlined against the Starlight X; set-down and idle sleep left out this early. Replaces: `targets`, `targets2`.
- `r1_zero` (intro) **Why lighter wins**: The reason weight matters, argued properly: theoretical ease of control and how fast you can change targets (less mass, less force to start and stop), not "overall speed". Teach it with a classroom demonstration (F = ma: a cart, a shopping trolley, a wrist flick). Replaces: `zerograms`.
- `r1_listed` (intro) **Listed against weighed**: The problem of misadvertised weights: brands list one weight and ship another. Carry the thesis's message ("optimization over opinion") here, as he asked. Replaces: `weightgap`.
- `r1_density` (intro) **Density, not weight**: The X2F's wrong idea first (the problem), then density as the fix: the size-weight illusion, why a low-density mouse feels lighter in the hand. Only outlines or silhouettes of the mice, never detailed drawings. Replaces: `ntr_density`, `ntr_sizewt`.
- `r1_rank` (intro) **Ranked by density**: Honest ranking among wireless mice: G-Wolves from EloShapes included even where Theseus looks worse; the X2F CrazyLight at its best-reported weight (at least 29.5 g); one precision for every mouse; the XA25 shown but not ranked (wired). Replaces: `ntr_densrank`.
- `r1_price` (intro) **Price against weight**: A Pareto front that never kinks (it continues its trajectory), with the Viper Mini Signature Edition, the Zaunkoenig mice and the Superstrike X3 added. Replaces: `pricefront`.
- `r1_budget` (intro) **Where 29.31 g goes**: The weight budget, computed from the deck's own data so it updates itself (his note: "a lot of work to keep updating this one"); the facts from ntr_stats that belong here. Replaces: `budget`, `ntr_stats`.
- `r1_close` (end) **Zero compromises**: The close: each compromise from r1_compromise, answered, in one frame. Replaces: `close`.
