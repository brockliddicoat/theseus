Read the model

Arrows show the input direction; the moving plungers show only the axial response. Each cap's center reaches the dashed line at its calculated switch threshold. These are local projection diagrams with enlarged travel, not mouse CAD or contact geometry.

Keep the claim bounded

At 20°, “6% sooner” belongs to the same-speed model. The same-force, zero-resistance model gives 3.0623%. Resistance, compliance, a changing trigger angle and fixed electronic delay change the result.

There is no measured Theseus latency, reaction-time advantage, friction loss or wear-life result here. Both models use the same axial actuation criterion.
