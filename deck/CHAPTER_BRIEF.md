## 3D
`view3D(key, t, x, y, w, h, state, opts)` (see `src/08-r3d.js`) composites a live three.js scene into the slide. Declare the scenes a slide uses with `S({ three: [...] })`. Scenes: `shell` (Theseus shell: `clickL/clickR`, `scroll`, `side0/side1`, `explode`, `xray`, `colorway`), `shell-sora` (the stock Sora shape, for before/after), `pcb` (`explode`, `xray`, `power` + `powerMode` 'charge'|'run' + `powerT`, `reflow`, `pressL/pressR`), `gt01` (`press`, `cutaway`, `explode`), `wheel` (`scroll`, `xray`, `explode`), `claw` (`insert`, `section`, `explode`), `screw` (`drive`, `section`, `explode`), `skates`, `battery` (`explode`), `lattice` (`cut`, `build`, `stress` + `stressCase`, `compare` 'tri'|'hex', `filletZoom`). Camera: `view` ('threeQuarter', 'top', 'front', 'left', 'right', 'back', 'bottom', 'low', 'threeQuarterRight'), `yaw`, `pitch` (radians), `zoom` (1 = framed; objects frame loosely, so 1.6–2.4 is typical), `target` [x,y,z] mm, `lift`, `fov`. Axes: mm, +Y up, +Z forward (nose), **+X = the user's LEFT**. Animate state and camera from `t` (deterministic: screenshots at a given t must repeat). The 3D builders are still refining the assets underneath; the scene names and state keys above are stable. Look at the asset READMEs in `3d/<asset>/README.md` for what each one shows. Don't edit anything under `3d/` and don't rebuild the bundle — tell the lead (in your report) what you need.
3D is a strong tool for the parts that are hard to draw (switch internals, PCB, the wheel, the lattice, the assembled mouse), but a precise 2D drawing or chart is still better for numbers. Every 3D shot needs 2D labels/callouts on top that say what to look at.

## Rules that keep parallel work safe
- **Edit only your own files** (listed in your task). Do not edit `01-core.js`, `03-parts.js`, `04-ui.js`, `07-charts.js`, `08-r3d.js`, `05-data.js`, `06-audio.js`, `12-00-ideas.js`, `13-sort.js`, `14-a-exdata.js`, `20-app.js`, `00-head.html`, `tools/*`, `DESIGN.md`. If you need a change there, write it in your report; meanwhile use a local helper.
- **Prefix every new top-level identifier** (function/const/let) in your files with your chapter code (e.g. `clk_`), so files can't collide when concatenated.
- **Sources:** add new ones in your own file with `Object.assign(SRC, { key: { t: 'Title: the specific claim', u: 'https://…', n: 'optional note' } })` near the top; prefix new keys with your chapter code. Reuse existing keys where they fit (`grep -n "^  [a-zA-Z0-9_]*: {" src/05-data.js`).
- **Slide ids** must stay unique across the deck; prefix new ones with your code. Use `after:`/`before:`/`replaces:` placement (see DESIGN.md) to put slides in order within your chapter.
- **Build and test only your own output**: `python3 tools/build.py main --out=<code>` writes `dist/main-<code>.html` (the shared `dist/assets/` serves the 3D files). Never build plain `main` (the lead's file). Screenshots: `node tools/shoot.js $PWD/dist/main-<code>.html shots/<code> settle <ids…>` and `mid`; `node tools/shot.js $PWD/dist/main-<code>.html shots/<code> <id>:<t> …`; contact sheets with `python3 tools/sheet.py`; layout QA with `node tools/qa.js $PWD/dist/main-<code>.html <ids…>`; runtime errors with `node tools/errs.js $PWD/dist/main-<code>.html <ids…>`; slide ids with `node tools/ids.js $PWD/dist/main-<code>.html`. Headless Chromium is shared: only kill processes you started, by PID.
- Look at your screenshots (settled and mid-motion) with the Read tool and iterate until each slide holds up to a close look on a phone (downscale to 844 px wide to check). Zero errors, zero QA issues at the end.
- Don't publish, don't commit, don't touch other chapters' files.
